## Open issues

- **CEC original page.** The candidate-list document was found only as a PDF print hosted on idi.org.il (title "ועדת הבחירות המרכזית לכנסת … הבחירות לכנסת ה-26", dated 09.09.2026). The original appears to be on a gov.il-style CEC page, but its URL was not found, and bechirot.gov.il returned no list content. If the reviewer can find the original URL on bechirot.gov.il (publisher `cec`) or www.gov.il, it should replace or supplement the IDI print. Note that the `cec` publisher's allowed host is only bechirot.gov.il; if the CEC now publishes on www.gov.il, a host-list data fix may be needed (sources.ts comment permits adding a host for an existing publisher).
- **Homat Torat Yisrael** is corroborated by the official print and only one news publisher (Ynet). A second independent outlet would raise confidence. Kan (kan.org.il) returned HTTP 403 and could not be opened.
- **Reforms and legislation were not exhaustively searched.** Not reviewed: programs of Housing Minister Yitzhak Goldknopf (Agudat Yisrael, 2022–2025), Housing Minister Litzman (2020–2021), Jerusalem and Heritage Minister Meir Porush (2023–2025), Litzman's government bills as Health Minister (2015–2017, 2019–2020), and private laws initiated by UTJ MKs (e.g., Uri Maklev, Moshe Gafni, Yaakov Asher). A second pass could check these against Knesset RIC and State Comptroller evaluations. On the evidence gathered, the section would show "Insufficient data".
- **Deputy ministers.** UTJ's member parties long held Health Ministry posts as deputy ministers, not ministers. §3.2 credits only "a minister from the party". This report applied the rule literally. The contributor may want to confirm that reading, since it applies to every party (§8).
- **Legal record.** No case was found for Yaakov Asher. The searches were web searches, not a full review of court databases. A reviewer may want to search court.gov.il decisions by name.
- **Ynet article dates.** The two ynetnews.com articles show no publication date on the page. From the content, b1e7ngq00gg is from early September 2026 and ryn11gcpige from mid-October 2026; these are inferences.
