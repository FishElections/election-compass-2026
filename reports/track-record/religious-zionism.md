## Open issues

- **ICC arrest-warrant request (2026).** TOI (2026-05-19) reports Smotrich "announced on Tuesday that the prosecutor's office of the International Criminal Court in The Hague has requested an arrest warrant be issued against him". Ynet (https://www.ynetnews.com/article/rkhzh0dyml, undated) relays a Middle East Eye report on a "sealed warrant request" and alleged crimes, and adds that the ICC denied issuing new warrants against Israelis. The TOI article is https://www.timesofisrael.com/smotrich-says-icc-has-issued-warrant-for-his-arrest-promptly-orders-demolition-of-west-bank-hamlet/. The request is sealed, the ICC has not confirmed it, and no source found reports a pre-trial chamber decision as of the snapshot, so the current status is unknown. The ICC (icc-cpi.int) is also not on the closed source list. Not included under §4. The reviewer must also decide whether an international (non-Israeli) proceeding is in §4.1 scope; the methodology does not say.
- **Atid Echad – Atid Tov LeYisrael** appears as a third member party on the CEC page only, as #3 Orit Strock's party. No approved news source confirming it was found. Since no achievements qualified, this has no effect on displayed data now, but a second source is advisable.
- **CEC host mismatch.** The 26th-Knesset candidate-list pages are on www.gov.il (departments/central-elections-committee), not bechirot.gov.il as `sources.ts` assumes for `cec`. The gov.il page returned 403 to WebFetch, so this report cites the IDI-hosted copy (publisher `idi`). Options: add www.gov.il to `cec` hosts (a data fix per sources.ts), and/or have a human open the gov.il page and cite it directly.
- **2005 case:** the court decision on the detention appeal (Justice Joubran, reported by Ynet) would outrank the news sources, but it was not located. The end date of the proceeding is not stated by any source. The case is supported by news only (2 independent publishers), so confidence is medium.
- **Achievements:** this pass found nothing qualifying. A deeper second pass could check Bank of Israel annual reports and State Comptroller reports for any measure led by Smotrich (Finance, 2022–; Transportation, 2019–2020), Uri Ariel (Tkuma; Housing 2013–2015, Agriculture 2015–2019) or Strock. Whether National Union–Tkuma ministers serving within earlier joint lists (Jewish Home, Yamina) count as "led by a member party" is a §2.3 question for the reviewer.
