## Open issues

- **Original CEC page not opened.** The leader is confirmed through an IDI-hosted printout of the CEC list page, plus Ynet. A verifier with browser access should locate the CEC's own 26th-Knesset list page (gov.il or bechirot.gov.il) and add it as the primary source.
- **Future-dated Knesset record.** Knesset OData already shows a 26th-Knesset Ra'am faction membership for Abbas starting 2026-11-10, after the snapshot and the election. It was not used. It may be a placeholder, and should be ignored or checked.
- **Electricity law (Amendment 136) needs a deeper check.** It is the strongest legislation candidate. A deeper search of Knesset Research and Information Center (knesset.gov.il/mmm) and State Comptroller publications for data on §157A(h) orders, or on homes connected before and after January 2022, could change its status. A Knesset Research and Information Center document from December 2020 (pre-law) appeared in search snippets but was not opened.
- **Other laws not searched in depth.** Other Ra'am-initiated laws were screened only. None was searched in depth for an evaluation; the default outcome is exclusion.
- **No legal-record cases.** Searches were limited to web search. A verifier could check Knesset immunity records and the Supreme Court ruling database by name, though nothing in this pass suggested a case exists.
