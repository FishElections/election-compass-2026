# Track record: review summary (methodology v1.6)

- **What this is:** a compiled view, per veteran party, of what the site would show under v1.6: peace and normalization agreements, reforms and laws with proven impact, major laws (impact not evaluated) and the leader's legal record. Compiled only from `verified/*.md`, `veteran-status.md` and the pass-1 party reports. No new research; no facts changed.
- **Snapshot date:** 2026-10-18 (every source accessed that day).
- **Eval result (`verified/eval-sample.md`):** 24 of 61 rows sampled (13 systematic + 11 high-stakes): 0 major errors, 2 minor (8.3%). Both minor errors and the dead RIC link have since been fixed in `legal.md` / `reforms-legislation.md`. With 0 of 24, the 95% upper bound on the major-error rate is about 12.5%. `major-legislation.md` was not in the sample and has had no second-pass check (§6 step 2).
- **Not shown:** diplomatic-relations-only agreements (v1.6), Nefesh Achat (excluded), Ra'am's Planning and Building Amendment 136 (failed the impact test).
- **Non-veteran parties** (yashar, amcha-yisrael, hamiluimnikim) show no track record: "The party has not yet served a full Knesset term."

"NEEDS SOURCE" = the item is credited through a joint-list member party that is inferred, not sourced (v1.6 §3.4). It must not ship until sourced.
