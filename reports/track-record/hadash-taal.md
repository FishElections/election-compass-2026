## Open issues

- **CEC primary page not located on the allowed host.**
  - The list was confirmed from an IDI-hosted printout of the CEC page (idi.org.il, publisher "idi"). Embedded links in that printout point to `https://www.gov.il/he/departments/central-elections-committee`, which suggests the CEC page lives on www.gov.il, not on bechirot.gov.il (the CEC host in `sources.ts`).
  - The verifier should try to find the CEC's own page for the list. If it is on www.gov.il, the CEC host list is a data fix. Citing IDI for a CEC publication is a judgment call; two independent news publishers corroborate it either way.
- **Hadash vs. registered name.** The CEC filing names the Israeli Communist Party (Maki), not "Hadash". News sources call it Hadash. The display name for crediting member-party items needs a decision for all three locales.
- **Legislation screen was not exhaustive.** About 550 initiator records were found, and only the most likely candidates were checked for evaluations. A later pass may find an approved-body evaluation for another law. The finding is "none located", not "none exist".
- **Clean Air Law initiator data.** The Knesset OData record for the passed bill (98122) does not list Dov Khenin. Interpreting the IsInitiator flag (True vs. null) needs confirmation if this law is revisited.
- **Legal record.** Only news and web searches were run. No court-database (court.gov.il) name search was possible. Nothing was found, so the section is not shown under §4.4.
- **Publication date of the Ynet agreement article** (`https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/syoll7qdgl`) is not displayed on the page, so it was not cited.
