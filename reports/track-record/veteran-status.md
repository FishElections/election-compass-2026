# Veteran status: research report

**Rule:** docs/track-record-methodology.md §2.4. A party is veteran if it,
including documented predecessors, held Knesset seats for a combined total of
at least **1,461 days** (four years under Basic Law: The Knesset, §8).
**Snapshot date:** 2026-10-18. Factions still serving are counted up to this date.
**Source:** the Knesset's official open-data service, table `KNS_Faction`
(knesset.gov.il). Each faction below links to its own official record, which
holds its name, Knesset number, start date and end date.

## How factions were matched
Each party is linked to factions by their **official ID number**, not by
searching names. A name search wrongly matched the 1977 "United Arab List"
(הרשימה הערבית המאוחדת, factions #369 and #419) to today's Ra'am. Those factions
are a different party and are **not** used.

For each veteran party, only the most recent factions needed to pass the
threshold are listed. Earlier seats exist for several parties, but they
don't change the result.

## Notes

1. **Joint List.** Every faction used carries the name "הרשימה המשותפת" (the Joint List).
   Its make-up changed over time: Ra'am was part of it in the 20th–23rd Knessets
   and is now a separate party. A source still has to confirm that the 2026 Joint
   List is made up of Hadash, Ta'al and Balad. The 20th-Knesset faction alone
   (1,491 days) passes the threshold.
2. **HaDemocratim.** Its Knesset faction in the 25th Knesset is still officially
   named "העבודה" (Labor, #1100, serving since 2022-11-15). If a source documents
   that HaDemocratim continues Labor (a rename or a merger, §2.3), Labor's seats
   count, and the party is veteran. Research pending.
3. **Beyachad** is led by Naftali Bennett and Yair Lapid (per
   `src/data/parties`). Its exact make-up, e.g. whether Yesh Atid runs inside it
   as a party, still needs a source. The methodology defines predecessors as a
   rename, a merger or a split (§2.3). It does not say whether a *joint list*
   inherits its member parties' Knesset time. Yesh Atid's 20th-Knesset faction
   alone (#904, 2015-03-31 to 2019-04-30, 1,491 days) would pass the threshold.
   **This needs a decision.**
4. **Hamiluimnikim.** Yoaz Hendel's former party, Derech Eretz, held seats for 465
   days in total (#954: 373 days, #1087: 92 days). That's under the threshold even
   if it were documented as a predecessor, so the result doesn't depend on it.
