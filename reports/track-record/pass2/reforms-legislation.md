## Open issues

- **likud, both rows provisional.** Neither row is a clean pass. The contributor should rule on:
  - whether "scale" (§3.2) can be met by indicator series or a market-size figure rather than a programme budget or beneficiary count;
  - whether a package of ministry measures (cellular) counts as a "national program";
  - whether minister-of-the-ministry is enough for "led by" when no official source names the minister personally.

  These rulings apply to every party (§8).
- **Kahlon / cellular reform: check result.** Approved sources confirm that Kahlon was Minister of Communications (2009-03-31 to 2013-03-18) and sat in the Likud faction in the 18th Knesset (Knesset OData). No approved official or evaluation source names Kahlon as the reform's leader. The Bank of Israel says "the government"; the State Comptroller says "the Ministry of Communications" and dates the process from 2007 (before his term). The common attribution to Kahlon appears in news coverage, which was not opened or cited here.
- **Open Skies, Decision 441 (2006).** Decision 59 continues Government Decision 441 of 2006-09-12. That decision was not opened, so its proposing minister and party are unverified. If the contributor treats the 2006 decision as the start of the reform, credit would be shared with the government of the time.
- **Sugar tax, peer-reviewed full text.** A verifier on another network (or with institutional access) should open https://doi.org/10.1016/j.puhe.2025.106118. The article is CC-BY 4.0 per Crossref. Confirm the study periods and the absolute sales figures, and check that nothing in the full text contradicts the abstract.
- **Sugar tax, instrument question.** Flagged, not decided: is a government decision implemented by a purchase-tax order a "government decision or national program" under §3.2? The government-decision part is now documented (Decision 263), which was the missing piece in pass 1.
- **Raam, Amendment 136.** Rejected on the RIC's own conclusion. If the contributor considers the number of §157A(h) orders (5 → 27) a valid indicator of the stated goal, the item could be reconsidered. The same RIC document reports that the goal's direct indicator (connections) barely moved, so §3.3(4) would likely still exclude it.
- **Not researched for Likud** (leads only): the 2003 economic plan and the 2005 Bachar capital-market reform (Finance Minister Netanyahu); the 2014 Food Law and other laws with Likud initiators; Likud-MK private laws with Knesset RIC follow-up papers. A systematic Knesset OData scan (KNS_BillInitiator joined to Likud-faction MKs, StatusID 118), cross-checked against RIC "יישום" papers, as done for Ra'am in pass 1, would be the next step.
- **Yisrael Beiteinu, other ministers.** Pass 1's list of other ministers (Aharonovitch, Landver, Shamir, Landau, Misezhnikov, Forer) was not re-researched in this pass.
- **Archive links:** none collected.
