# Major legislation, impact not evaluated (methodology v1.3 §3.4 item 3)

Snapshot and access date: 2026-10-18. Every URL in this file was opened on 2026-10-18 (HTTP 200 with content; 134 URLs). OData URLs are fetched with `$format=json`. Plenum protocols (`.doc`) and bill PDFs were downloaded with curl and read as text (macOS textutil / PDFKit). No archive links were collected. Researcher: automated pass. It still needs the §6 independent re-check and contributor review.

## Method and what was scanned

**Rule applied (identically to every party).** Up to 3 laws that passed third reading and that the party *initiated* or *sponsored*.
- **Initiated:** an MK of the party appears in `KNS_BillInitiator` with `IsInitiator = true` on a bill whose `SubTypeDesc` is "פרטית" (private).
- **Sponsored:** for a government bill (`SubTypeDesc` "ממשלתית"), the person who presented it at the first-reading plenum debate is a minister from the party. Deputy ministers don't count.
- **Not credited:** committee bills ("ועדה"). Also not credited: initiators of other bills merged into the passed bill, who are not on the passed bill's record (see Open issues).
- **Order:** Basic Laws first, ties to the newest. Then the largest cost estimate stated in the official explanatory notes, ties to the newest.
- **"Passed"** means `KNS_Bill.StatusID = 118` ("התקבלה בקריאה שלישית"). The date shown is the plenum session where the bill had status 117 ("לדיון במליאה לקראת קריאה שלישית"), from `KNS_PlmSessionItem` + `KNS_PlenumSession`. The Reshumot date is `KNS_Bill.PublicationDate`.

**Party of a person (§2.3).** The party is the person's Knesset faction (`KNS_PersonToPosition.FactionID`) on the relevant date: the third-reading date for Basic Laws, the Reshumot date for step 2 and the first-reading date for presenters. Factions were matched to parties by official faction name or ID:
- likud: names containing "ליכוד".
- yisrael-beiteinu: "ישראל ביתנו".
- shas: "ש"ס" / "התאחדות הספרדים".
- yahadut-hatorah: "יהדות התורה".
- religious-zionism: "הציונות הדתית".
- otzma-yehudit: #1081, #1106.
- kachol-lavan: #946, #969, #1110.
- hadash-taal: Hadash factions (#391 … #1067, named "חזית דמוקרטית לשלום ושוויון"/"חד"ש"); Ta'al #22, #602, #1054, #1057, #1062, #1070, #1076, #1090; Balad #657, #20, #51, #626, #744, #802, #1060, #1068.
- raam: #16, #52, #1053, #1059, #1069, #1075, #973, #1099.
- hademocratim: Labor factions ("העבודה…", "מפלגת העבודה הישראלית") and Meretz ("מרצ").
- beyachad (Yesh Atid only): #812, #904, #950, #967, #1102.

When the faction on the date was a **joint faction**, the MK's member party was taken from the MK's nearest single-party faction in the Knesset data, and the row says so. Joint factions: the Joint List #903/#940/#943/#1074/#964/#1089, the Ra'am–Ta'al and Ra'am–Balad lists, Blue and White #917/#938/#1079, Yesh Atid–Telem #955 and National Unity #1085/#1098. A minister who was not an MK on the date (Norwegian law) was handled the same way.

**Step 1: Basic Laws.**
- `KNS_Bill` returned **7,752** bills with StatusID 118 across all Knessets. Of these, **130** have a name starting with "חוק-יסוד" (Basic Laws, Basic-Law amendments and Basic-Law temporary provisions): 57 private, 45 government, 28 committee.
- Cross-check: `KNS_IsraelLaw` (`IsBasicLaw eq true`, 19 Basic Laws) → `KNS_LawBinding` gives 140 laws. Of these, 130 are the same set. The other 10 are ordinary laws that amend a Basic Law (e.g. "חוק הממשלה (הוראות מעבר)") and were not counted as Basic Laws.
- Initiators were read for all 57 private Basic-Law bills. They were 149 persons, and each one's factions were read from `KNS_PersonToPosition`.
- The first-reading presenter was read from the plenum protocol ("דברי הכנסת") for **22 of the 45** government Basic-Law bills, which is every one passed from 2013 on. They are 480036, 480895, 488358, 564215, 564808, 568148, 2006018, 2007950, 2025778, 2025749, 2072205, 2087322, 2139663, 2142765, 2143630, 2143986, 2146354, 2151572, 2159660, 2159659, 2203822 and 2210751. Presenters found:
  - Finance Minister Smotrich (RZ): 2203822.
  - Finance Minister Lapid (Yesh Atid): 480895.
  - Welfare Minister Shmuli (Labor): 2146354.
  - Finance Minister Katz (Likud): 2143630.
  - Interior Minister Sa'ar (then Likud): 488358.
  - Justice Ministers Livni, Shaked and Sa'ar (parties outside this list): 480036, 568148, 2025778, 2025749, 2072205, 2159660 and 2159659.
  - Finance Minister Kahlon (Kulanu): 2007950.
  - Deputy ministers, not credited: Cohen (Shas), Akunis (Likud) and Woldiger (RZ).
- The other **23** government Basic-Law bills (1960–2010) were **not checked**. That doesn't change any result:
  - Each party with 3 items has all three newer than 2010.
  - Otzma Yehudit, Kachol Lavan and Yesh Atid had no Knesset seats before 2013.
  - No Hadash, Ta'al, Balad or Ra'am MK has ever held a minister post in `KNS_PersonToPosition`. Every person in the factions listed above for hadash-taal (51 persons) and raam (39 persons) was checked.
- Result: likud, yisrael-beiteinu, shas, yahadut-hatorah, religious-zionism and hademocratim have 3 or more Basic Laws. kachol-lavan has 2, beyachad 2, otzma-yehudit 1, and hadash-taal and raam have 0.

**Step 2: cost estimates (only for parties with fewer than 3 Basic Laws).**
- Candidate laws:
  - **Private laws:** every passed private bill with an initiator from the party, found through `KNS_BillInitiator` for every person in the party's factions.
  - **Government laws** (only for parties with ministers): a presenter was looked for in the first-reading protocol of all **682** government laws of the 19th, 23rd, 24th and 25th Knessets. A presenter was found for 552. These are the only Knessets in which Otzma Yehudit, Kachol Lavan or Yesh Atid held ministers.
- For each candidate, the first-reading bill PDF (`KNS_DocumentBill`, "הצעת חוק לקריאה הראשונה") was converted to text. Sentences with cost wording ("עלות", "העלות התקציבית", …) and a shekel amount were extracted. They were then read from the top down until the open slots were filled.
- Only an explicit statement of **that bill's own** budget cost was accepted. A statement belonging to another bill in the same booklet did not count. Neither did background figures, allocations, savings, fines or per-person amounts.

**Not scanned:**
- The preliminary-reading (פ/) versions of private bills.
- "Background material" files.
- Image-only PDFs (text extraction may return nothing).
- Laws whose initiators are missing from `KNS_BillInitiator`.
- 130 government laws of the 19th, 23rd, 24th and 25th Knessets whose presenter was not found automatically. The 84 of them that have a first-reading PDF were cost-scanned anyway. Their highest matches were a background figure (11.5 billion NIS, the cost of an existing tax benefit), the cost of a government decision, a loan amount and a saving. None was a statement of the bill's own cost above the figures selected below. The other 46 have no first-reading PDF and were not scanned.
- Earlier names were excluded because §2.3 needs a continuity source, which was not researched: Maki/Rakah before 1977 (Hadash), Mada factions (Ra'am), Otzma LeYisrael #1051 (18th Knesset) and Herut/Gahal (Likud).

## Open issues

1. **Basic-Law amendments counted as Basic Laws.** This report treats every law whose name starts with "חוק-יסוד" as a Basic Law. That includes amendments and temporary provisions, such as the State Economy and budget Basic Laws. Counting only original Basic Laws would change almost every party's list. The contributor should rule on this.
2. **Committee bills.** Committee bills (SubType "ועדה") are not credited, even when `KNS_BillInitiator` lists MKs on them.
   - Example: 2223298 "חוק-יסוד: הממשלה (תיקון מס' 13)", third reading 2025-05-12. It lists Eliyahu Ravivo (Likud), Efrat Rayten Marom (Labor) and Karine Elharar (Yesh Atid).
   - If credited, it would become #1 for hademocratim and for beyachad. For beyachad it would replace 579102. Likud is unaffected.
3. **Merged bills.** Government bill 488358 "חוק-יסוד: משאל עם" was presented by Interior Minister Gideon Sa'ar, then in Likud. Knesset data also lists Yariv Levin, Ayelet Shaked and Orit Strock as initiators, from the merged private bill P/1551/19. Under this report's rule only Sa'ar is credited. This doesn't change any party's top 3.
4. **Deputy ministers presenting.** Deputy Finance Minister Yitzhak Cohen (Shas) presented 2087322, 2139663, 2142765, 2143986, 2151572, 564808 and 2006018. Deputy Finance Minister Michal Woldiger (RZ) presented 2210751 (Basic Law State Economy, amendment 14, 2023-12-14). None is credited. If deputies counted, Shas's #3 would become 2151572 (2020-12-28). RZ's list would not change.
5. **Stand-in presenters.** Some ministers presented bills of another ministry and are credited under the literal rule. Example: hademocratim #1, 2146354, where Shmuli (Labor) presented instead of the Deputy Finance Minister. The Otzma Yehudit ministers Eliyahu and Wasserlauf also presented several other ministries' bills, but none was selected. If "sponsored" should mean only the responsible minister, hademocratim becomes 2087638, 540974 and 132198 ("חוק-יסוד: הכנסת (תיקון מס' 38)", 2007, MK Shelly Yachimovich, faction "העבודה-מימד בראשות עמיר פרץ"). That last item's member party would need a check, because it is a joint faction.
6. **Member party inferred, not sourced.** In the following rows the faction on the date was a joint faction, and the member party was inferred from the MK's single-party factions in Knesset data. A source on member-party affiliation (e.g. the CEC lists) is still needed.
   - hadash-taal #1 (579102). Abdullah Abu Maaruf, also an initiator, has no single-party faction and was not assigned.
   - raam #1 (579102). If it is rejected, raam's #3 becomes 167796 ("חוק לתיקון פקודת מס הכנסה (מס' 121) (הוראת שעה)", 2000, 60 million NIS revenue loss), initiated by Taufik Khatib and Muhammad Kanan, faction #16.
   - kachol-lavan #3 (2210669). If it is rejected, the next kachol-lavan item would need re-checking.
7. **UTJ crediting by member party.** §2.3 credits joint lists' member parties by name, but `KNS_PersonToPosition` gives only the faction "יהדות התורה". The member party (Degel HaTorah or Agudat Yisrael) of Gafni, Asher, Pindrus and Tesler is not sourced here.
8. **How cost figures are compared.** The deterministic convention used here is the largest single shekel figure stated as the bill's cost. A range counts at its upper bound, and one-time and annual figures are not normalised.
   - Under an "annual cost" convention, otzma-yehudit #3 would change. 2228138 (151 million NIS per year) would rank below 2210669, which states 156 million NIS for 2024 and also has Otzma Yehudit initiators.
   - hadash-taal #2 and #3 would swap under a lower-bound convention. The set of three stays the same.
9. **What counts as a "cost estimate".** Allocations and background figures were not accepted.
   - 2146987 (National Insurance amendment 218, presented by Justice Minister Nissenkorn, Kachol Lavan) mentions a 940 million NIS allocation and a 4.341 billion NIS figure for a different measure.
   - 2192650 (presented by Welfare Minister Meir Cohen, Yesh Atid) mentions a 2 billion NIS addition.
   - If allocations counted, kachol-lavan #3 would become 2146987. beyachad would not change.
10. **IsInitiator flag.** 142245 lists Mohammad Barakeh, Hana Sweid (Hadash) and Talab al-Sana (Ra'am) with an empty initiator flag. They were not counted. This doesn't change any selection.
11. **Same-name Ra'am.** The 15th-Knesset faction #16 "רע"ם" is treated as today's Ra'am under the §2.3 same-name rule. The 1977 United Arab List factions (#369, #419) are excluded.
12. **Future-dated Knesset rows.** `KNS_PersonToPosition` already contains factions dated 2026-11-10 (e.g. #1114, #1120, #1121), after the snapshot date. They were not used for any attribution in this file.
