## reform-open-skies-2013 (likud)

- **Verdict: KEEP, pending one contributor ruling on scale.** Every other element checks out. The CBS traffic check (below) shows Israeli airlines' share fell because foreign carriers grew faster, while total traffic and Israeli carriers' own flights both rose.
- **Led by (sourced):** Yisrael Katz, Minister of Transport, National Infrastructure and Road Safety.
  - **Decision 59** (2013-04-21), §א.2, authorizes "שר החוץ ושר התחבורה, התשתיות הלאומיות והבטיחות בדרכים לחתום על ההסכם וליישם אותו" (gov-il | https://www.gov.il/he/departments/policies/2013_des59 | 2026-10-18).
  - **Signing on 2013-06-10 in Luxembourg:**
    - Times of Israel: "Transportation Minister Yisrael Katz and Civil Aviation Authority Director Giora Rom" (times-of-israel | https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-signs-open-skies-deal-with-eu/ | 2026-10-18).
    - Globes English, 2013-06-11: the agreement was signed by Katz and Leo Varadkar for the EU (globes | https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1000851526 | 2026-10-18).
  - **Initialling on 2012-07-30:** ynetnews quotes Katz on approving the deal; the initialling itself was by Giora Rom (ynet | https://www.ynetnews.com/articles/0,7340,L-4262219,00.html | 2026-10-18).
  - **Office (Knesset OData KNS_PersonToPosition):**
    - 3429: 2009-03-31 to 2012-01-25.
    - 3463: 2012-01-25 to 2013-02-05.
    - 23373: 2013-03-18 to 2013-08-25. This covers both the decision and the signing.
  - **Party:**
    - Faction 28804 (18th Knesset) is Likud.
    - **In April–June 2013 Katz sat in the joint faction "הליכוד - ישראל ביתנו"** (28948, 2013-02-05 to 2014-07-09). See Open issues.
  - **Shared origin:** Decision 59 opens with "בהמשך להחלטת הממשלה מספר 441 מיום 12.9.2006". Decision 441, made by the 31st government (Olmert), set the policy of negotiating a single EU aviation agreement. It gave the steering team to the Transport Ministry Director-General (gov-il | https://www.gov.il/he/departments/policies/2006_des441 | 2026-10-18). Decision 441 names no minister as the lead.
  - The 2013 approval, signing and implementation authority rest with Katz.
- **Nationwide:** yes. It is a national aviation agreement that replaces Israel's bilateral agreements with EU states. The BoI paper describes the removal of limits on carriers and frequencies between any EU point and Israel.
- **Scale (what exists, sourced):**
  - **Budget / beneficiaries / units for the reform itself:** none found in an official source.
  - **Only money figure in Decision 59:** an extra NIS 5 million a year toward Israeli airlines' security costs (§ב.1), summer 2015 to winter 2016–17. This is a side measure.
  - **Market-size figures (CBS, all Israeli airports):**
    - International passengers (arriving + departing): 12,515 thousand in 2012 and 13,559 thousand in 2013 (CBS Statistical Abstract 2014, table 24.8, https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/DocLib/2014/ShnatonTransportAndCommunications/st24_08.xls).
    - International passengers later: 20,463 thousand (2017), 22,646 thousand (2018) and 24,358 thousand (2019).
    - Aircraft movements: 81,180 (2010), 131,610 (2017), 145,634 (2018) and 152,904 (2019) (CBS Statistical Abstract 2020, table 19.9, https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/DocLib/2020/19.ShnatonTransportandRoadSafety/st19_09.xls, published 2020-07-26).
    - CBS does not split these by EU / non-EU route, so none of them measures "the reform's scale" directly.
  - **BoI impact figure:** departures of Israelis per capita rose 51.2% beyond the growth explained by the other factors in its model (2013q3–2018q4). This is an impact estimate, not scale.
  - The contributor must decide whether a market-size figure satisfies "scale".
- **Stated goal (sourced):** Decision 59 §א: "במסגרת יישום מדיניות הממשלה להגברת התחרות בתחום התעופה האזרחית לישראל וממנה". Decision 441's title is "עידוד התחרות בתחום התעופה האזרחית לישראל וממנה".
- **Indicator:** Israeli airlines' share of aircraft movements (the incumbents' share), as a measure of competition.
- **Before:**
  - "35%-כ ערב הרפורמה" (BoI, chart 1, 12-month moving sum of CBS aircraft movements; the series starts in 2011).
  - CBS cross-check, computed by the verifier from table 19.9 (not a published ratio): 38.0% in 2010.
- **After:**
  - "מעט פחות מ-30% בעקבותיה" (BoI). The paper does not give an exact end date; its chart runs to 2019.
  - CBS cross-check (verifier's computation): 32.8% (2017), 29.7% (2018), 29.0% (2019).
- **Direction:** down. Competitors gained share while total traffic grew, which matches the goal.
- **Evaluation source(s):**
  - **Primary:** bank-of-israel | https://www.boi.org.il/media/cuod3wrf/%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%A7%D7%A8-%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A4%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%97%D7%99%D7%9D.pdf | 2026-10-18. This is the BoI Economic Spokesperson paper of 2021-10-17. In its summary the reform "הגבירה את התחרות בענף הטיסות". It says the agreement came into force gradually "החל מאפריל 2013" and reached full implementation in March 2018.
  - **Supporting:** knesset-ric "אגרות רשות שדות התעופה – תיאור וניתוח", 2017-03-19 (https://fs.knesset.gov.il/globaldocs/MMM/d514d9c6-3ddb-e611-80cc-00155d0206a2/2_d514d9c6-3ddb-e611-80cc-00155d0206a2_11_9734.pdf). It says the Open Skies agreement brought new competitors, more routes and lower fares, citing a Civil Aviation Authority report on 2011–2014 price falls. The percentages are garbled in the PDF text layer and were not used.
- **Conflicting evidence:**
  - None of equal standing found.
  - The same BoI paper finds no significant effect on incoming tourists ("לא נמצאה עדות אמפירית להשפעת הרפורמה על כניסות תיירים"). Incoming tourism is not the goal stated in Decision 59 or 441, so this is not opposite evidence.
  - No State Comptroller or Knesset RIC evaluation reporting the opposite was found in a search of those hosts.
- **Repeal:** none.
- **Card text suggestion:** "Government Decision 59 (April 2013) approved the Israel–EU 'Open Skies' aviation agreement, which Transport Minister Yisrael Katz signed in June 2013; the Bank of Israel reports that Israeli airlines' share of aircraft movements fell from about 35% before the reform to a little under 30% after it, while the number of flights grew."

## Open Skies — total traffic check

**Question:** did the Israeli airlines' share fall because competition grew, or because total flights fell?

**Answer: total traffic rose sharply, and Israeli airlines' own traffic also rose. Their share fell because foreign carriers grew faster.** Total flights did not fall.

## Open issues for the contributor

1. **Scale (§3.2), Open Skies and cellular.** Neither has an official programme budget or beneficiary count. The sugar tax does have an official budget-type figure: estimated NIS 380m a year and about NIS 900m actual in 2022.
   - **Open Skies:** CBS market totals, about 12.5m international passengers in 2012 and 24.4m in 2019.
   - **Cellular:** cellular companies' revenues of NIS 19bn in 2010. Per the Comptroller's footnote this figure includes equipment sales.
   - Decide whether a market-size figure satisfies "scale", and apply that ruling to every party (§8).
2. **Cellular "led by" rests on news only.** Four independent publishers (Haaretz, Times of Israel, Calcalist, Globes) name Kahlon as having led the reform. No official or evaluation source names him: BoI says "the government" and the Comptroller says "the Ministry of Communications". Decide whether news is acceptable for the "led by" element of §3.2. §3.1 allows "2 independent sources"; §3.2 is silent.
3. **Cellular instrument.** It is a package of licence amendments, a frequency tender and an Economic Policy Law chapter, not one government decision. Decide whether this counts as a "national program" under §3.2.
4. **Open Skies, joint faction.** At the decision (April 2013) and signing (June 2013), Katz sat in the joint Knesset faction "הליכוד - ישראל ביתנו" (OData 28948). He is a Likud member (Likud faction in the 18th and 24th Knessets), and §2.3 (v1.1) credits joint-list items to the member party by name. Confirm the credit to Likud.
5. **Open Skies, shared origin.** Decision 441 (2006, Olmert government) started the policy. Decision 59 (2013) approved the agreement and authorized Katz to sign and implement it. Decide whether the card should mention the 2006 start.
6. **Sugar tax, preliminary evaluation and confounders.** The BoI analysis is labelled preliminary, and untaxed drinks also fell net of trend in its own table. Consider putting this in the item's ⓘ. No equal-standing source reports the opposite, so §3.3(4) is not triggered.
7. **Sugar tax, Liberman's party link while minister.** His Yisrael Beiteinu faction record in the 24th Knesset ends 2021-06-15, two days after he became minister. He was minister as Yisrael Beiteinu's leader, but no single official page tying "Finance Minister" to "Yisrael Beiteinu" was opened. The faction record and the 26th-Knesset list (pass 1) cover it indirectly.
8. **Archive links:** none collected for any URL in this file.
9. **Selection (§3.4):** if all three reforms are kept, the order newest first is: sugar tax (2022), Open Skies (2013), cellular (2010–2012). Likud would have 2 items and Yisrael Beiteinu 1.
