## Open issues

- **Search budget exhausted (affects C and D).** The session-wide web-search limit was reached partway through the legal-record research. Reforms (§3.2) and legislation (§3.3) were therefore not researched, and both sections read "Insufficient data" for that reason, not because nothing qualifies. Leads for a follow-up pass (none checked against the §3.2/§3.3 evidence test):
  - The cellular-market reform led by Communications Minister Moshe Kahlon (then Likud), 2011–2013: licensing of new operators and the ban on exit fees.
  - Capital-market reform ("רפורמת בכר") and the 2003 economic plan led by Finance Minister Netanyahu (2003–2005).
  - Any law initiated by a Likud MK that has a Knesset RIC or Bank of Israel evaluation.
- **§2.3 continuity for peace-egypt-1979.** Crediting the 1979 treaty to today's Likud needs an approved source documenting that the current party continues the 1977 Likud (then an alliance of parties that later merged into one party). The Knesset records name the faction "הליכוד" in both periods, but a name match is not one of the §2.3 grounds. Until this is sourced, the row should not ship.
- **§3.1 scope ruling needed (affects all parties).** Read literally, "an agreement that establishes … normal diplomatic relations" also covers routine establishments or restorations of relations. During Likud premierships these include China and India (1992), the USSR (1991), South Sudan (2011), Chad (2019) and Kosovo (2021, included above with a flag). None of these except Kosovo were researched. The contributor should decide whether §3.1 means peace/normalization treaties only (Egypt, UAE, Bahrain, possibly Morocco) or all diplomatic-relations instruments. That is a methodology clarification (§8) and must be applied to every party.
- **Somaliland (2025-12-26).** Netanyahu and Foreign Minister Gideon Sa'ar signed a joint declaration of mutual recognition with Somaliland's president, which provides for full diplomatic relations, ambassadors and embassies. Verified in times-of-israel (https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-becomes-first-country-to-recognize-breakaway-somaliland-as-independent-state/, accessed 2026-10-18) and jpost (https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-881475, accessed 2026-10-18, which quotes Sa'ar: "the establishment of full diplomatic relations"). Not put in the table because whether Somaliland is "another state" under §3.1 is contested: Israel was the first country to recognize it, and Somalia, Egypt, Turkey, Djibouti and Saudi Arabia objected. This needs a contributor ruling.
- **Morocco (peace-morocco-2020) classification.** The 2020 declaration resumed contacts and committed both sides to full relations, at liaison-office level. A possible later Israel–Morocco step (upgrade to embassies, reported by ynetnews around September 2026) was not verified as a signed agreement. If such an agreement was signed during the current Netanyahu government, it would also be credited to Likud.
- **Israel–Lebanon agreement of 17 May 1983** (signed while Begin was PM). It ended the state of war but was not ratified and was abrogated by Lebanon in 1984. It was not researched or sourced in this pass, and whether it qualifies under §3.1 is unclear.
- **Pardon request.** Netanyahu submitted a pardon request to President Herzog in November 2025 (the article does not state the year; November 2025 is inferred from the article dates). On 2026-04-26, Herzog said he would not consider the request yet and called for plea-deal talks first: times-of-israel (https://www.timesofisrael.com/herzog-pushes-for-new-plea-deal-talks-says-he-wont-consider-netanyahu-pardon-request-yet/) and ynetnews (https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h1auauo6zg, page undated). No later decision was found up to the snapshot, but the search budget ran out, so a decision between May and October 2026 cannot be ruled out. Nothing is shown for the pardon unless a decision is sourced. The legal row's "on trial" status is independently supported by October 2026 reports.
- **AG final decision of 2019-11-21** is supported by ynet only. It needs a second independent outlet or the Justice Ministry's gov.il notice before it can be shown as a step.
- **Other past matters not included.** I could not establish from approved sources whether a criminal investigation (as opposed to a preliminary examination) was opened against Netanyahu personally, or how it ended, in these matters:
  - the "Bibi-tours" travel-funding matter: HCJ 8192/14 Rosenthal v. AG exists, but the decisions read concern the normative guideline and not a criminal file;
  - the Prime Minister's Residence expense matters;
  - the Milikowsky shares matter.
  These matters are left out, per the rule for statuses that cannot be established.

That date is after the snapshot and after the 2026-10-27 election. It was not used for anything; it looks like a pre-populated or placeholder record. The veteran-status count should not pick it up.
