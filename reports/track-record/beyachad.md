**Note A (form of the alliance).** Approved sources describe the arrangement in different ways:
- **One merged party:** Walla and Calcalist on 2026-04-26; Times of Israel on 2026-09-02 ("merged earlier this year with Bennett's new Bennett 2026 party").
- **A joint slate of two parties that keep their names:** Walla on 2026-09-06; JPost on 2026-09-06.

Under §2.3 both forms give the same result: a merger in which Yesh Atid joined, or a joint list with Yesh Atid as a member party. Either way, Yesh Atid's Knesset time counts.

- **Reforms and legislation not exhausted.** No Yesh Atid-led item was carried through the full §3.2 or §3.3 evidence test. Promising directions for pass 2, all requiring a Knesset source and an evaluation body with before/after figures:

  - Yesh Atid ministers in the 36th–37th governments (2021–2022): Economy (Barbivai), Energy (Elharrar), Welfare (Cohen), Social Equality (Meirav Cohen), Tourism (Razvozov), and Lapid as PM.
  - Re-run the Knesset OData query for laws initiated by Yesh Atid MKs, with working pagination, and restrict it to bills initiated while the MK sat in a Yesh Atid faction.
- **Form of the alliance (note A).** Sources differ between "merged party" and "joint slate of two parties". The veteran result is the same either way under §2.3, but a human should confirm the wording shown to visitors. The Ynetnews filing report (https://www.ynetnews.com/article/syywdl3oge) quotes a Beyachad representative saying Bennett decided "against a merger for electoral reasons". The context suggests a merger with other parties (e.g. Eisenkot's), but this was not confirmed.
- **Yesh Atid still inside the list as of the snapshot.** The latest approved sources confirming Yesh Atid on the slate are from 2026-09-02 to 2026-09-07, around the 2026-09-08 filing deadline. The CEC's approved list, with member parties and candidate order, could not be opened (bechirot.gov.il returned 404; the gov.il CEC pages returned 403).
- **Faction #1086 "ביחד" (2022).** The Knesset records a faction named "ביחד" with Bennett as its only member (2022-08-15 to 2022-09-06). This is a name match only. It was not counted, and no source links it to the 2026 party or to Bennett 2026.
- **Turkey 2022.** Whether a restoration of full diplomatic relations without a signed text can ever count under §3.1 is a methodology reading that a human should confirm. It was excluded here.
- **Personal data in an official source.** The gov.il Registrar notice used for Bennett 2026's registration lists founders' home addresses. It is cited only for the registration fact. Nothing personal from it should be reproduced on the site.
