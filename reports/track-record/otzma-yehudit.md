## Open issues

- **Foreign proceeding scope.** The Italian investigation (`italy-flotilla-investigation`) is the only open proceeding found, and it is foreign and news-sourced only. The human reviewer should confirm that §4.1 ("criminal proceedings against the leader personally") covers foreign prosecutors' investigations. If it does not, the legal section would show only concluded proceedings.
- **Aggregate legal row.** The court ruling describes 15 indictments from 1993–2007 only in aggregate. §4.2 asks for each case separately. Splitting them needs case-level court decisions (for example Jerusalem Magistrates' and District Court files), which are not public on court.gov.il for those years, or 2 independent outlets per step. The verifier should decide whether one aggregate row sourced to a Supreme Court ruling is acceptable, or whether it should be relabelled "convicted (final)". That relabel would rest on the 2021 register deletion and the ruling's "last conviction 2007", which is an inference and was therefore not made here.
- **Count inconsistency inside HCJ 8910/22.** Para. 2 says 13 convictions out of 15 indictments; para. 19 says "15 הרשעות". The row uses para. 2, which is attributed to the AG's response.
- **State Attorney's 2024 incitement-investigation request.** No outcome found in approved sources, so it is excluded. A gov.il announcement or later reporting might settle it.
- **No Central Elections Committee source for the list.** The leader is confirmed via 2 independent news outlets only. If the committee's published list (bechirot.gov.il) becomes reachable, it should replace or supplement them.
- **Firearm licensing reform.** It was not evaluated in depth. If revisited, State Comptroller reports on firearm licensing (library.mevaker.gov.il) and the Knesset RIC document "היבטים ברישוי כלי ירייה פרטי בישראל" should be read in full. They are likely to bear on §3.3(4) (conflicting evidence) rather than to support a "goal met" finding.
